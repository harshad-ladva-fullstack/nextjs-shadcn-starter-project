## MVP Scope - Phase 1 Features

### 1. Phase 1 Objective

Build an AI-powered Business Intelligence System that acts as a guide and advisor for business owners.

**Core Principles:**
- Focus on clarity, insights, and decision support
- Prove value fast so users trust the system daily
- Minimal UI/UX - Less artificial, more intelligent

### 2. Core Philosophy for Phase 1 Architecture

- **Insight-first**, not data-heavy
- AI should explain, not overwhelm
- Visual dashboards over tables
- Opinionated guidance (system has a point of view)
- Modular architecture for future blockchain integration

### 3. User/Business Onboarding

**Quick onboarding process (Very Important):**

- Email + password signup
- Business profile creation
- GST-based company lookup (auto-fetch basic details)
- Manual fallback if GST not available
- Business type selection (Retail, Wholesale, Manufacturing, Service, etc.)
- Basic configuration (currency, region, tax type)

### 4. Data Input & Sources

**MVP Scope:**

- Manual data entry (simple forms)
- CSV upload (sales, inventory, purchases)
- POS/billing system data import (limited connectors or CSV-based)
- Daily/weekly data sync (manual trigger for MVP)

### 5. Business Health Dashboard

**Core Screen (Home Screen):**

- Overall Business Health Score
- Revenue trend (daily / weekly / monthly)
- Profit direction (up / down / flat)
- Inventory efficiency indicator
- Cash stress indicator (basic logic)
- Customer repeat ratio

**Design Principles:** All visual. Minimal numbers. Clear signals.

### 6. AI-Powered Insights Engine

**Core Value - System-generated insights:**

- Top-performing products
- Slow-moving or dead inventory
- Revenue drop or spike explanations
- Margin warnings
- Sales pattern observations
- Seasonality hints

**Insights Requirements:**
- Short
- Contextual
- Action-oriented
- Written in plain language

### 7. Inventory Intelligence Module

**High Impact Features:**

- Current stock visibility
- Fast-moving vs slow-moving items
- Overstock warnings
- Reorder suggestions (range-based)
- Inventory aging indicators

### 8. Forecasting & Business Trajectory

**Light ML Implementation:**

- Sales forecast for next 7 / 30 days
- Inventory demand prediction
- Trend comparison with previous periods
- Growth or decline direction indicators

**ML Models (Initial):**
- Moving averages
- Time-series trend detection
- Pattern matching

### 9. AI Decision Assistant

**Text-Based Interface:**

- Chat-style interface
- Predefined business questions + free text
- AI answers using business data + logic
- No hallucinations - strictly data-backed responses

**Example Questions:**
- "How is my business doing?"
- "What should I reorder?"
- "Why are profits falling?"
- "Which product should I focus on?"

### 10. Visualization & UX Layer

**Design Standards:**

- Clean charts (line, bar, donut)
- Color-coded indicators
- Minimal text density
- No accounting jargon
- Consistent layout across modules

### 11. Feedback & Learning Loop

**Beta Only:**

- In-app feedback prompts
- Insight usefulness rating
- AI response quality feedback
- Anonymous usage analytics

**Purpose:** Continuous improvement of models and UX

## Summary

**MVP Goal:** Deliver an insight-first, AI-powered business intelligence system that provides clear, actionable guidance to business owners with minimal complexity and maximum value.
